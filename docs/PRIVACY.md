# Privacy Notice (template draft)

_Last updated: 2026-11-18. This is a template-based draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer._

**Short version: we do not collect your data.**

- **Holdings, cash, settings, alerts, tranche plans, your watch list and target prices, imported option chains, checklist notes and API keys** are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to PixelBlue LLC.
- **Data requests** go directly from your browser to the providers you configure (for example Finnhub for quotes and news, Twelve Data for daily prices, Anthropic for AI). Those providers receive your API key and the symbols or text needed for each request, under their own privacy policies.
- **Calendar export** (.ics) is generated in your browser and saved as a file on your device.
- **Automatic refresh**: while the page is open and the US market is open, your browser asks your quote provider for prices at the interval you choose (default every 5 minutes). You can turn this off.
- **AI features** use Anthropic with your own key, only when you press a button or send a question: AI explanations and questions send a summary of your holdings for that day (symbols, weights, daily moves, related headlines); sector explanations send the sector's move, its top holdings' moves and recent headlines; translation sends the news titles and summaries being shown.
- **Links to quote websites** (Yahoo Finance, Google Finance or TradingView, as you choose) open those sites in a new tab. Portus does not fetch data from them; the site you open applies its own privacy policy.
- **No analytics, no cookies, no tracking** are included in the free edition.
- **Hosting**: the web app is served as static files (GitHub Pages). GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under its own policy.
- **Deleting your data**: use Settings → "清除全部" (clear all), or clear your browser's site data.
